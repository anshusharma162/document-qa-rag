# Northwind Analytics – Travel and Expense Policy

Version 4.1 · Owner: Finance · Applies to all business travel and reimbursable expenses

## 1. Approval

All business travel must be approved in the travel portal by the employee's manager before booking. International travel additionally requires approval from the department head at least 21 days before departure.

## 2. Air travel

Domestic flights must be booked in economy class. International flights longer than 8 hours may be booked in premium economy. Business class requires written approval from the CFO.

Flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance where possible. Employees should choose the lowest logical fare within a two-hour window of their preferred departure time.

## 3. Rail and local transport

Train travel is preferred for journeys under 6 hours. AC 2-tier is the standard class for overnight trains.

For local travel, app-based cabs are reimbursed at actual cost. Use of a personal car is reimbursed at ₹12 per kilometre and a two-wheeler at ₹6 per kilometre, based on the distance logged in the expense tool.

## 4. Hotels

Nightly hotel limits, including taxes:

| City tier | Limit per night |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune) | ₹7,500 |
| Tier 2 cities | ₹5,000 |
| Other locations | ₹3,500 |
| International | USD 200 |

Bookings above the limit require the manager's approval with a reason, for example a conference hotel.

## 5. Meals and daily allowance

A daily meal allowance is paid instead of itemised meal claims:

- Domestic travel: ₹1,200 per day
- International travel: USD 60 per day

If meals are provided by the client or the conference, the allowance is reduced by one third for each meal provided. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## 6. Submitting claims

Expense claims must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the expense date, with an itemised receipt for every item above ₹500. Claims submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed except with CFO approval.

Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll if approved before the 20th of the month.

Lost receipts may be replaced by a signed missing-receipt declaration, at most twice per year.

## 7. Corporate card

Employees who travel more than four times per quarter may apply for a corporate card. Personal spending on the corporate card is not allowed; any accidental personal charge must be repaid within 15 days.

## 8. Non-reimbursable items

Examples: fines and penalties, personal entertainment, upgrades not approved in advance, travel insurance already covered by the company policy, and expenses for accompanying family members.
