# LedgerLite – Product FAQ for Sales and Support

LedgerLite is Northwind's cloud invoicing and accounts-receivable product for small and mid-sized businesses.

## Plans and pricing

**What plans are available?**
There are three plans. Starter costs ₹999 per month and includes up to 100 invoices per month and 1 user. Growth costs ₹2,999 per month with unlimited invoices, up to 5 users and automated payment reminders. Enterprise is priced per quote and adds single sign-on, a dedicated success manager and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes. Every new account gets a 21-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card is required to start the trial.

**Is annual billing discounted?**
Annual billing gives two months free, which is roughly a 16% discount compared with paying monthly.

**Can customers change plans?**
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

## Features

**Which payment methods can invoices accept?**
Invoices include a payment link that accepts UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and, on the Growth plan and above, international cards in USD, EUR and GBP.

**Is LedgerLite GST compliant?**
Yes. LedgerLite generates GST-compliant invoices, supports e-invoicing with IRN generation for eligible businesses, and exports GSTR-1 ready reports.

**Does it integrate with accounting software?**
LedgerLite has two-way sync with Tally and Zoho Books, and a one-way export to QuickBooks. A REST API is available on Growth and Enterprise plans with a limit of 600 requests per minute.

**How do payment reminders work?**
On Growth and Enterprise, reminders are sent automatically by email and WhatsApp at 3 days before the due date, on the due date, and 7 days after it. The schedule can be customised.

## Data and security

**Where is customer data stored?**
All data is stored in data centres located in India (Mumbai region) with daily encrypted backups kept for 35 days.

**What happens to data after cancellation?**
After cancellation, data remains available in read-only mode for 90 days so the customer can export it. After 90 days it is permanently deleted.

## Support

**What support is included?**
Starter includes email support with a response within 2 business days. Growth includes chat and email support with a 4 business-hour response time. Enterprise customers get 24×7 phone support and a named success manager.

**What is the refund policy?**
Monthly plans are not refundable. Annual plans can be cancelled within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
