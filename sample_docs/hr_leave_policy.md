# Northwind Analytics – Leave and Attendance Policy

Version 3.2 · Owner: People Operations · Applies to all full-time employees in India

## 1. Working hours

The standard work week is Monday to Friday, 40 hours. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST, during which employees are expected to be reachable. Outside core hours, employees may plan their schedule flexibly with their manager's agreement.

Employees working in a hybrid arrangement must be present in the office at least two days per week. Team leads decide which days are anchor days for their team.

## 2. Annual (earned) leave

Every full-time employee earns 1.5 days of annual leave for each completed month of service, which equals 18 days per calendar year. Leave accrues from the date of joining.

A maximum of 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward into the next calendar year. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 December. Carried-forward days must be used by 30 June of the following year, otherwise they also lapse.

Annual leave of more than three consecutive working days must be requested at least 14 calendar days in advance through the HR portal. Shorter leave should be requested at least two working days in advance.

Employees may encash up to 5 days of unused annual leave per year at their basic daily salary. Encashment requests are processed in the December payroll.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees receive 8 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry forward and cannot be encashed.

A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for sick leave of more than two consecutive days. The certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal within three working days of returning to work.

If sick leave is exhausted, further absence due to illness is deducted from annual leave, and then treated as unpaid leave.

## 4. Casual leave

Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per year for personal matters. Casual leave cannot be taken for more than two consecutive days and cannot be combined with annual leave to extend a vacation.

## 5. Parental leave

Birthing parents are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. Up to 8 weeks may be taken before the expected delivery date.

Non-birthing parents, including adoptive parents, are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

After returning from maternity leave, employees may work from home for up to 3 months with manager approval.

## 6. Public holidays

The company observes 12 public holidays per year. The list is published by People Operations every December for the following year. Employees may swap up to 2 regional holidays for festivals they observe, provided the request is made at least one week in advance.

## 7. Unplanned absence

If an employee cannot work due to an emergency, they must inform their manager before 10:00 IST on the same day. Three unreported absences in a rolling 12-month period lead to a formal review.

## 8. Notice period and leave

Annual leave cannot be used to shorten the notice period without written approval from the department head. Unused annual leave at exit is paid out in the full-and-final settlement, up to a maximum of 30 days.
